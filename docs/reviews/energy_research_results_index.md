# Research results and artifact index

The historical controller outputs listed below used the copied raw benchmark dataset. The current study configurations now use `data/farms/curated/reviewed_ghi_2025_to_2026_08`; new PV models are still being developed separately. The short January and August controller windows are retrospective development comparisons. They do not establish annual performance or select a controller for deployment. Actual auction/settlement publication assumptions and data snapshots are recorded with each run; observed snapshot timestamps do not prove historical availability.

- `outputs/energy_price_forecasts/chronological_2026_v3/summary.md`: 30/30 chronological price fits, with January and August 2026 evaluations. Day-ahead training starts in 2024, whereas these baseline intraday and settlement fits start in 2025.
- `outputs/energy_price_forecasts/extended_2024_training_v2/summary.md`: 27/27 fits including October 2025 and 2024 hourly intraday training data. The 2024 intraday observations are coarse training targets, not native quarter-hour products. October IDA3 includes all 31 days after the DST lag fix.
- `outputs/research_inputs/pv_calibration/calibration_summary.md`: older raw-target calibration, retained for traceability. Its results are not the current reviewed-target study.
- `outputs/research_inputs/pv_calibration_reviewed/calibration_summary.md`: four January-fitted bias/spread models assessed against reviewed targets in January and August without refitting. On the two selected August days, calibration worsened CRPS for all four farms. Farm 910's August target contains 132 historical-GHI pvlib values and 60 policy-defined night zeros, so it is a counterfactual rather than an independent meter-based score.
- [Reviewed PV replay inputs](reviewed_pv_replay_inputs.md): all-farm source counts, retained exclusions, checksum and retrospective reconstruction rules.
- `outputs/energy_research/legacy_continuous_validation_v1/`: 32 completed January/August development jobs. The [independent audit](continuous_mpc_validation_legacy_v1.md) found 18 excessive imbalance-cap residuals across two oracle jobs; those old results are retained as evidence, not treated as corrected.
- `outputs/energy_research/ensemble_oracle_corrected_v3/`: eight corrected ensemble-oracle jobs. The [independent audit](continuous_mpc_validation_ensemble_oracle_v3.md) found no cap or physical/accounting violations. Four August jobs each retained 40 time-limited feasible stage-one solutions; no grid-tracking fallbacks were used.
- `outputs/energy_research/economic_continuous_development_v1/`: five farm-1 development jobs. The [audit](continuous_mpc_validation_economic_v1.md) reconstructed physical and cash quantities and checked 192 conditional MPC horizons per target controller without violations.
- `outputs/energy_research/new_research_pilot_v3/`: four farm-1 two-day development jobs using calibrated ECC, calibrated shuffle, causal fundamental-price forecasts, and continuation value. The [audit](continuous_mpc_validation_pilot_v3.md) checked 192 conditional horizons per target controller and found no physical or cash-accounting violations. These are not the planned complete matrix.
- [MPC stage-one performance investigation](mpc_stage1_performance.md): recorded 51-member problem dimensions, relaxation/alternative-formulation diagnostics, gap-qualified fallback, and independent ten-second stage-budget benchmark. Horizon forecast cash values must not be read as realized profit.

Completed replay directories retain their original manifests and source paths. If a manifest refers to a `/tmp` snapshot path, that is the recorded location at execution; the directories listed here are the preserved copies. Model artifacts and replay outputs are intentionally ignored by Git because of their size; the source, configurations, tests and concise review files are committed. The original PV-development checkout is separate and was not included in these commits.

The full 26-case study and the unfinished May 2026 deterministic-oracle replay were not complete at the time of this index. Their status must be taken from their own manifests, not inferred from the presence of configuration or output files. Re-run matched comparisons on the reviewed dataset and, when available, the new PV models, preserving exact dataset/model identifiers.
